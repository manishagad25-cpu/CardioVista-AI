# CardioVista AI — Limitations, Ethical Considerations & Medical Safety

## 1. Primary Medical Disclaimer

> **CARDIOVISTA AI IS AN EDUCATIONAL AND RESEARCH PROTOTYPE.**  
> Its predictions do not represent a clinical medical diagnosis and have not received clearance or approval from regulatory authorities (FDA, CE, EMA, or CDSCO). It must never be used to make diagnostic decisions, direct invasive interventions, modify prescriptions, or delay certified clinical care.

---

## 2. Dataset Scope & Cohort Limitations

### 2.1 Sample Size Constraints
The model was trained and evaluated on the **Extension of Z-Alizadeh Sani Dataset** consisting of **303 patients** from the Tehran Heart Center. While 303 records represent a valuable benchmark for academic study, machine learning models trained on small cohorts exhibit statistical vulnerabilities:
- High sensitivity to outliers and small distributional shifts.
- Potential overfitting on sub-strata (e.g. rare comorbid conditions like CRF or CVA which have fewer than 10 occurrences in the dataset).

### 2.2 Single-Center Demographic Bias
All records originate from a single cardiovascular referral center in Tehran, Iran:
- **Demographic Representation:** Genetic predispositions, dietary habits, and baseline cardiovascular risk profiles in this cohort may not generalize to diverse global populations.
- **Referral Bias:** Patients scheduled for catheterization angiography generally have high pre-test probability of CAD (71.3% CAD prevalence in this dataset). The model will overestimate risk if applied to unselected, screening-level asymptomatic populations.

## 3. Methodological & Model Limitations

### 3.1 Non-Causal Feature Contributions
Feature importance scores generated by tree models and linear models represent **statistical associations** within the training distribution:
- Correlation does not equal biological causation.
- For example, high ESR (erythrocyte sedimentation rate) correlates with CAD in the model due to systemic inflammation, but lowering ESR with anti-inflammatory agents does not directly unblock a stenosed coronary artery.

### 3.2 Imbalance in Branch Stenosis Outcomes
While CAD and LAD have relatively balanced positive classes (71.3% and 58.4%), LCX and RCA stenosis classes have lower positive prevalence (39.3% and 37.6% respectively):
- Cross-validation F1-scores for LCX (0.5983) and RCA (0.6374) reflect lower sensitivity compared to LAD (0.8056) and primary CAD (0.8957).
- This indicates greater uncertainty when predicting isolated circumflex or right coronary branch lesions without anatomical imaging.

### 3.3 3D Heart Representation Limitations
The WebGL heart model is an **illustrative anatomical visualization**:
- It demonstrates coronary branch territories and highlights predicted risk zones.
- It is **not** a patient-specific 3D segmentation derived from coronary CT angiography or invasive fluoroscopy.
- Anatomical variations (e.g. left-dominant vs. right-dominant coronary circulation, ramus intermedius branches, or anomalous origins) are not captured in the default standard anatomical model.

---

## 4. Recommendations for Future Work

1. **Multicentric Validation:** Evaluate the pipelines against independent external cohorts (e.g. Cleveland Heart Disease, Framingham Heart Study, UK Biobank).
2. **Longitudinal Risk Tracking:** Incorporate serial biomarkers and post-revascularization follow-up records.
3. **Multimodal Deep Learning:** Integrate 12-lead raw ECG waveform signals and echocardiography video loops directly via convolutional and attention architectures.
